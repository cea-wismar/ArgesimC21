Models DIsc2.slx, DIas.slx, DIesu1.msim use the short cut, approximated
Shockley and "explicite Shockley" diode. For DIas the number of breakpoints
N = 5.

The Matlab script explicitShockley.m executes three tasks:
- It runs all three models with ode45 and ode4 and compares the results. 
- It runs the variants DIesu1 and DIesi1 of the explicit shockley with ode4 and
  compares the results.
- It runs the variants DIesu1 and DIesi1 with ode4 and DIshu1 with ode45 and
  plots results and rel. errors in explicitShockley.pdf.
